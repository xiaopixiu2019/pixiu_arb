# Community code of conduct

We want this project to be a technically rigorous and welcoming place to collaborate.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and reviews with respect, patience, and specific technical evidence;
- welcome questions and different experience levels;
- accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and keep criticism focused on the work;
- protect other people's privacy and never publish credentials or sensitive trading data; and
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and sustained personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when practical, and protect the safety and privacy of affected people.

Report conduct concerns through the same private channel described in `SECURITY.md`. Do not include credentials, account data, or unrelated private information.
